                  Boost & Slide

                  Hold the key you're already moving in to speed up — it ramps up, so keep it held (a quick tap does nothing).
                  Turning while boosting turns your head instantly, but your body keeps skidding sideways — the faster you were going, the longer the skid — and there's no mercy at walls while boosting. Let off boost first for a clean corner.

                  🪅 Piñata bounties

                  Grow a really long snake and you become a piñata: when a big snake dies, it bursts into a wide scatter of gold candy near where it fell — a sudden feeding frenzy, and the drop leans toward whoever's shortest. The gold decays fast, so dive in and scoop it before it vanishes (and before someone else does). Multiplayer only.

                  ⚠️ Grid decay

                  The board isn't static: the server drops warning cells that turn solid a few seconds later — watch for the pulsing orange tile. Once it's spikes, it's a permanent-feeling wall: touch it and you die just like hitting the edge of the board. Each one despawns on its own after a while, so the layout keeps shifting instead of a corner ever staying safe forever.
